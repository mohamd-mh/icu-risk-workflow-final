What happens next?

  1. The model aligns entered values to the input schema.
  2. Missing values are handled by imputation.
  3. The AI model estimates risk probability.
  4. The result panel shows SIRS screening, confidence, suggested priority, and recommended action.
  5. The coordinator opens the created review ticket, documents the case, and prepares it for quality-team review.
