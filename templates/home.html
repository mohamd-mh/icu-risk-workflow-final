What the system lets users do

ICU Browse ICU cases

Move from dashboard overview into a searchable worklist of extracted ICU stay records.

HIGH Prioritize high-risk stays

Use risk level, data quality, review status, and local priority to organize review work.

VIEW Open assessment

Compare baseline and multi-agent outputs, risk drivers, validation notes, and workflow trace.

NOTE Save review notes

Save local status, priority, reviewer initials, and notes without changing prediction logic.
