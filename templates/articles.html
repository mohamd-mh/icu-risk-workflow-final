Planning-Driven Programming: A Large Language Model Programming Workflow

Main idea: LPW shows how a task can be broken into planned steps before the final answer or program is created.

Connection to our project: Our multi-agent workflow can first plan the risk assessment process, then assign clear steps to agents such as validation, risk assessment, and explanation.

AutoGen: Enabling Next-Gen LLM Applications via Multi-Agent Conversation

Main idea: AutoGen presents a way for multiple agents to work together through structured conversation.

Connection to our project: Our project uses the same idea at a simple level: different agents can check data, estimate risk, verify the result, and create an explanation.
