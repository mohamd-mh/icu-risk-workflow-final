Layer 2

SIRS-style Screening Baseline

This is a transparent screening baseline used for comparison and review context. It is separate from the AI model and from outcome prediction.

  • Temperature above 38C or below 36C
  • Heart rate above 90/min
  • Respiratory rate above 20/min
  • White blood cell count above 12,000/mm3 or below 4,000/mm3

SIRS Positive means 2 or more of the implemented criteria are met. The workbench displays the flag so coordinators can compare a transparent screen with the trained model output.

Dataset Basis

Dataset / Preprocessing Basis

patient_features_ai.csv is the processed 1000-row dataset used by the final model. Earlier patient_features_real.csv was a 100-row development output.

Relevant raw MIMIC-III tables are PATIENTS.csv, ADMISSIONS.csv, ICUSTAYS.csv, D_ITEMS.csv, D_LABITEMS.csv, CHARTEVENTS.csv, and LABEVENTS.csv. Raw files are not included because of size and access restrictions. Regenerating the dataset may change model metrics and requires retraining/retesting.

Boundaries

What the System Does Not Claim

  • Does not replace clinicians or qualified reviewers.
