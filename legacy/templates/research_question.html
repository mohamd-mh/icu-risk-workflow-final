Research Question

How does a planning-driven multi-agent LLM workflow, inspired by LPW and AutoGen, affect the accuracy, reliability, and transparency of early ICU risk prediction on a real extracted subset of MIMIC-III compared with a baseline single-pipeline approach?

Independent Variable

The independent variable is the workflow type. The project compares a baseline pipeline with a planning-driven multi-agent workflow.

Dependent Variables

  • Accuracy: how well the system estimates the ICU stay risk level.
  • Reliability: how well the system handles missing or unusual data.
  • Transparency: how clearly the system explains its result and process.

Expected Output

  • Risk level: low, medium, or high.
  • Explanation: a short reason for the risk result.
  • Workflow trace: a record of the steps used to reach the result.
