Research Question

How does structuring AI-based ICU risk prediction as a modular, role-separated software workflow - rather than a single opaque model - affect the reliability, transparency, and auditability of the resulting system, compared with a single-pipeline baseline?

Independent Variable

The independent variable is the workflow type. The project compares a single-pipeline baseline with a modular, role-separated software workflow.

Dependent Variables

  • Reliability: how well the system handles missing or unusual data.
  • Transparency: how clearly the system explains its result and process.
  • Auditability: how well the system's steps, decisions, and history can be traced and reviewed after the fact.

Expected Output

  • Risk level: low, medium, or high.
  • Explanation: a short reason for the risk result.
  • Workflow trace: a record of the steps used to reach the result.
